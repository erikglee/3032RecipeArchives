## Instructions

1. Remove the outer leaves from the napa cabbage. Quarter each cabbage while keeping the core intact, then rinse the quarters.
2. Salt the individual cabbage leaves and massage them. Continue massaging every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
3. Make the slurry: boil the water, sugar, and glutinous rice flour together, whisking until incorporated. Let the mixture cool to room temperature before continuing.
4. Blitz the ginger, onion, and garlic in a food processor.
5. Once the cabbages have been salted, cut the cores from each quarter. Cut each cored quarter into three lengthwise sections.
6. Once the slurry is at room temperature, mix it with the Korean pepper flakes and the ginger-onion-garlic mixture.
7. Combine all ingredients, then transfer the kimchi to a crock.

## Fermentation notes

Ferment in the crock for 9 days. This year, noticeable bubbling did not begin until about day 4, so visible activity may take a few days to appear. After fermentation, bottle the kimchi and refrigerate it.

## Notes

This was a pretty good amount of cabbage. Next time, a small amount more could be nice, but this quantity was close to perfect.
