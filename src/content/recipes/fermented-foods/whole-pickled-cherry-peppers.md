  - 1 1/2 cups 5% acidity vinegar, white or rice vinegar
  - 1 1/2 cups water
  - 31 g kosher or pickling salt
  - 2 1/4 teaspoons sugar
  - Garlic cloves, optional
  - Peppercorns, optional

## Make and cool the brine

1. Start a day ahead if possible, so the brine can cool completely before it is poured over the peppers.
2. Combine the vinegar, water, salt, and sugar. Warm only enough of the liquid to dissolve the salt and sugar, then combine with the remaining cool liquid.
3. Cool the brine to room temperature or colder; refrigerate it overnight for the best texture.

## Pack the peppers

1. Wash and dry the cherry peppers. Use a clean skewer or paring knife to poke a few small holes in each pepper so the brine can penetrate.
2. Pack the peppers into clean jars with garlic or peppercorns, if using.
3. Cover completely with the cooled brine. Refrigerate immediately.

## Pickle and serve

1. Start tasting after 5 days; a week gives the brine time to reach the centers of the whole peppers.
2. Keep refrigerated and use clean utensils. The final texture and flavor are excellent: firm, tangy, and decidedly spicy.

## Notes

These are especially good sliced over pizza or stirred into salads. It would be interesting to try additional variants with herbs, warm spices, or different pepper varieties while keeping this same simple base brine.
