ingredients:
  - 1 cup fine bread crumbs
  - 2 1/2 cups milk, divided
  - 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  - 1/2 pound ground pork
  - 1 cup finely chopped onion
  - 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  - 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  - 3 to 4 teaspoons freshly ground nutmeg
  - 1/2 cup butter or margarine
  - 1/4 cup flour
  - 2 to 3 beef bouillon cubes
  - 1 1/2 cups hot water
  - 1 1/2 cups light cream
---

## Make the meatballs

1. Soften the bread crumbs in 1 cup of the milk.
2. Add the ground beef, ground pork, onion, eggs, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Mix thoroughly, then shape into 1-inch meatballs. An ice-cream scoop is useful for portioning.
3. Heat the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Brown the meatballs in batches, turning them until browned on all sides. Transfer the browned meatballs to a plate.

## Make the cream gravy

1. Stir the flour into the skillet drippings and cook, stirring, until blended.
2. Dissolve the bouillon cubes in the hot water. Gradually add the bouillon to the flour mixture, stirring constantly until smooth.
3. Stir in the remaining 1 1/2 cups milk and the light cream. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, for about 3 minutes or until smooth.

## Finish and serve

1. Return the meatballs to the gravy. Simmer gently for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until cooked through.
2. For the family card's baked finish, transfer the meatballs and gravy to an oven-safe dish and bake at 350F for 30 minutes before serving.

## Family notes

- The typed card's "f;pir" is flour.
- Use a 3:1 ratio of ground beef to ground pork: 1 1/2 pounds beef and 1/2 pound pork for this batch.
- The card calls for 1 teaspoon nutmeg (or allspice), but this family version uses 3 to 4 teaspoons of nutmeg. Fresh nutmeg makes a notable difference; replace an older jar when its aroma has faded.
