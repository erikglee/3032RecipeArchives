ingredients:
  - 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  - 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 8 to 12 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  - 4 to 5 garlic cloves, minced
  - 1/2 cup dry white wine
  - 3 cups chicken broth
  - 2 to 3 large handfuls spinach
  - 1 to 2 teaspoons fresh thyme leaves
  - 1 tablespoon butter
  - 1 lemon
  - Olive oil, if needed
  - Kosher salt and lots of black pepper
  - Red pepper flakes, optional
  - Parmesan, optional

## Crisp the chicken

1. Pat the chicken thighs very dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Set the thighs skin-side down in a cold skillet. Turn the heat to medium and cook without moving them for 25 minutes, until the skin is deeply golden and crisp.
3. Flip and cook for 5 to 7 minutes more, then transfer the chicken to a plate. Leave the rendered fat in the skillet.

## Brown the mushrooms

1. Turn the heat to medium-high. Add the mushrooms in an even layer and leave them alone for 2 to 3 minutes before stirring.
2. Continue cooking for 3 to 4 minutes, until deeply browned. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook until softened, about 4 to 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the garlic, thyme, black pepper, and red pepper flakes, if using. Cook for 30 to 60 seconds.

## Build the broth

1. Pour in the white wine and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer until reduced by about half, 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Add the chickpeas and chicken broth. Bring to a simmer, then smash about one-quarter to one-third of the chickpeas against the side of the pan with a spoon or potato masher.
3. Simmer uncovered for 12 to 15 minutes, until the broth is lightly thickened.

## Finish and serve

1. Stir in the spinach until just wilted, 1 to 2 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir in the butter, juice of half the lemon, a little zest, and plenty of black pepper. Taste and adjust the salt and lemon.
2. Nestle the chicken into the skillet skin-side up, keeping the broth below the skin. If needed, finish in a 400 F oven for 5 to 10 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165 F at its thickest point.
3. Serve the broth in shallow bowls with a chicken thigh on top. Finish with more lemon, thyme, Parmesan, olive oil, and crusty bread.

## Notes

For an extra savory finish, stir 1 to 2 teaspoons of Dijon mustard into the broth just before serving.
