  - 1 1/2 to 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into large bite-size pieces
  - 1/2 cup full-fat plain Greek yogurt
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  - 7 garlic cloves, divided, grated or minced
  - 2 1/2 tablespoons fresh ginger, divided, grated
  - 3 teaspoons garam masala, divided
  - 2 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin, divided
  - 2 teaspoons ground coriander, divided
  - 1 to 1 1/2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder or mild paprika, divided
  - 1 teaspoon ground turmeric, divided
  - 2 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
  - 1 tablespoon neutral oil, plus more for cooking
  - 2 medium garden tomatoes, roughly chopped
  - 1 can diced tomatoes, 14 to 15 ounces
  - 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  - 4 tablespoons butter, divided, plus 1 tablespoon cold butter optional
  - 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  - 1 to 2 teaspoons sugar or honey
  - 3/4 to 1 cup heavy cream
  - 1 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves, crushed
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom or seeds from 3 green cardamom pods, optional
  - Water, as needed

## Marinate the chicken

1. Combine the chicken, yogurt, lemon juice, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tablespoon ginger, 1 1/2 teaspoons garam masala, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder or paprika, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, 1 1/4 teaspoons salt, and 1 tablespoon oil.
2. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or 2 to 8 hours for more flavor.

## Char the chicken

1. Heat a large skillet, cast-iron pan, grill, or griddle until quite hot. Add a little oil and cook the chicken in batches for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until it has dark browned or charred spots but is not necessarily cooked through.
2. Transfer the chicken to a plate. Keep the browned bits in the pan.

## Make the tomato sauce

1. Lower the heat to medium. Add 2 tablespoons butter and the onion. Cook for 7 to 10 minutes, until very soft and beginning to turn golden.
2. Add the remaining garlic and ginger; cook for 1 minute. Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring frequently, for 2 to 3 minutes until it darkens to a rusty red.
3. Add the remaining garam masala, cumin, coriander, chili powder or paprika, turmeric, and optional cardamom. Cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add the garden tomatoes, canned tomatoes, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cook uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, until the fresh tomatoes break down and the sauce is concentrated.
5. Blend the sauce until completely smooth with an immersion or countertop blender. Return it to the pan.

## Finish the butter chicken

1. Stir in the cream, remaining 2 tablespoons butter, fenugreek leaves, and 1 teaspoon sugar or honey. Taste and add more sugar or honey if the tomatoes are especially tart.
2. Add the chicken and any accumulated juices. Simmer gently for 5 to 8 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through. Add water a few tablespoons at a time if the sauce is too thick, or simmer uncovered if it is too thin.
3. For an especially glossy finish, turn off the heat and stir in 1 tablespoon cold butter. Serve with basmati rice or naan and a small squeeze of lemon at the table.

## Notes

The defining details are true browning on the chicken, well-cooked tomato paste, and kasuri methi. The sauce keeps well and often tastes even better the next day.
