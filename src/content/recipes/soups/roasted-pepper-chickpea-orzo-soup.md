## Cook the chickpeas

1. Add the dried, unsoaked chickpeas and 3 cups water to an Instant Pot. Seal the lid and cook at high pressure for 35 minutes.
2. Let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes, then carefully release any remaining pressure. Drain the chickpeas and set aside. They should be tender but still hold their shape.

## Roast the vegetables

1. Heat the oven to 425F. Toss the bell peppers, onion wedges, and carrots with 2 tablespoons olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
2. Roast until the vegetables are tender and browned at the edges, 25 to 30 minutes, turning once halfway through. Set aside; the roasted vegetables are added to the soup later.

## Make the soup

1. If using bacon, cook it in a soup pot or Dutch oven until crisp. Transfer the bacon to a plate, leaving about 1 tablespoon of fat in the pot. If not using bacon, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil in the pot instead.
2. Add the celery with a pinch of salt and cook until softened, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic, coriander, turmeric, and red pepper flakes; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Add the broth, cooked chickpeas, and roasted bell peppers, onions, and carrots. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.
4. For a lightly creamy texture, blend 1 to 2 cups of the soup and return it to the pot, or mash some of the chickpeas against the side of the pot.
5. Add the orzo and simmer until tender, 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in the lemon zest, lemon juice, and most of the basil. Taste and adjust with salt, pepper, and more lemon juice as needed.

## To serve

Ladle into bowls and finish with the remaining basil. Sprinkle with the optional crisp bacon for a smoky, salty contrast.

## Notes

- Basil is the lead herb here; use it generously and add it at the end so it stays fresh and aromatic.
- The bacon is entirely optional. The soup is complete without it, but the salty crunch is a nice addition.
- The recipe begins with dried, unsoaked chickpeas; do not use canned chickpeas for this version.
