  - 1 medium head cauliflower, cut into florets
  - 2 to 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  - 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  - 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  - 1 teaspoon smoked or sweet paprika
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
  - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, plus more to taste
  - 1 cup Greek yogurt
  - 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  - 1 to 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  - 2 to 3 tablespoons pistachios, finely crushed
  - 1 to 2 tablespoons fresh basil, finely chopped
  - Aleppo pepper or cayenne, optional

## Roast the cauliflower

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the cauliflower with 2 to 3 tablespoons olive oil, the salt, cumin, paprika, coriander, turmeric, black pepper, and Aleppo pepper or cayenne, if using.
2. Spread the cauliflower on a sheet pan with the cut sides facing down where possible.
3. Roast for 25 to 35 minutes, tossing once, until deeply browned and tender. Squeeze a little lemon juice over it while hot.

## Make the yogurt base

1. In a bowl or mortar, combine the yogurt, garlic, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt. Taste with a piece of cauliflower and add more salt, up to 1/2 teaspoon total, if needed.
2. Stir in the crushed pistachios and basil. The yogurt should be thick enough to spread; thin it with lemon juice, water, or olive oil one teaspoon at a time only if needed.

## Serve

1. Spread the yogurt mixture thickly across a serving plate.
2. Pile the roasted cauliflower on top. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil, more pistachios or basil, and a pinch of Aleppo pepper if desired.
