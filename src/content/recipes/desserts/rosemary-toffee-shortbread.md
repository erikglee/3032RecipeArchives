## Make the toffee

1. Melt the brown sugar, butter, and water in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until dissolved.
2. Bring to a boil. Cook, swirling the saucepan occasionally, until the bubbles slow and the mixture reaches 290 F, about 6 to 8 minutes.
3. Pour onto a parchment-lined baking sheet. Let cool completely, then break or cut into small pieces.

## Make the cookies

1. Rub the orange zest and finely chopped rosemary into the sugar until it feels like wet sand.
2. Cream the sugar mixture and softened butter until fluffy.
3. Add the flour and mix only until just combined.
4. Fold in the crushed toffee.
5. Roll the dough between two sheets of parchment to about 1/4 inch thick.
6. Chill for 30 minutes, then cut into shapes with a favorite cookie cutter or a 2-inch round cutter.
7. Bake at 365 F for 16 minutes, until the bottoms are golden.

## Notes

Cool completely before eating so the toffee can set. The homemade toffee is adapted from this [NYT Cooking toffee recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/761573017-toffee); store-bought toffee works nicely when you want to skip the homemade batch.
